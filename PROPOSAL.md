# TerAustralis Proposal

**What should be built**

### 1. Southern Recovery & Industrial Node (Pilbara / Port Hedland axis)

- Leverage existing deep-water port and heavy-industry infrastructure
- Enable Starship recovery operations off Australian coasts with towing to Pilbara ports
- Develop large-scale energy storage (lithium battery pathways) and manufacturing capacity to support power and potential propellant logistics
- Expand dual-use tracking and re-entry coordination capability already present in the region

### 2. High-Latency Intelligence Layer — CrystalCore OS

- Local-first Edge AGI designed for intermittent connectivity and contested bandwidth
- Fail-safe state is local isolation, not fail-open to external systems
- Consent tokens gate significant actions and memory modification
- Continuity of decision-making and personal/operational memory treated as a hard architectural constraint
- Selective lattice synchronisation only under explicit consent

This intelligence layer matches the physical reality of multiplanetary and deep-outback operations.

### 3. Remote-Ops & ISRU Testbed Capacity

- Utilise Pilbara and Arkaroola-class terrain as Earth-based Mars analogues
- Support remote operations training, oxygen/methane pathway testing, and high-latency procedure development without leaving the planet

### 4. Strategic Alignment

- Position Australia as the southern redundancy and force-multiplier node under existing AUKUS and Technology Safeguard frameworks
- Create distributed infrastructure that reduces single-hemisphere concentration risk

### 5. Immediate Practical Path

1. Technical review of CrystalCore Edge AGI specification against high-latency / remote-ops requirements
2. Exploratory discussion on Pilbara / Port Hedland recovery, power, and feedstock pathways
3. Joint assessment of Southern Hemisphere ISRU and remote-ops testbed potential

No broad open-ended funding request. First: alignment of physics, geography, and architecture.
