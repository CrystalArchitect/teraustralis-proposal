Catch

Cadence without a catch is only speed. Catch, on this sitting, is three industrial jobs: recover the vehicle, refurbish it, and fuel it again. It is not a film still of arms around a booster. It is not a pad we inherit. It is not a contract.

Three jobs, one word

Recover Get a returning stage or ship out of the air or the water in one piece, with the heat shield and engines inspectable. Tower catch, runway, and ocean recovery are different machines. This sitting does not select among them.
Refurbish Inspect, replace, recertify. A vehicle that cannot be turned around is a throwaway with extra steps. Rapid reuse is the cost model; it is also a workforce, a hangar, and a spare-parts geography.
Fuel Methane and oxygen at scale, subcooled, next to a berth that can actually load. Propellant is a plant, not a slogan. Feedstock and energy have to exist on this continent if the south is more than a splash zone.

What this sitting proposes

A southern catch is geography plus plant: a coast with a deep-water port and heavy industry already there; methane and oxygen that can be made and stored; a workforce that can inspect steel that has been to space; and a consent process that starts with Traditional Owners if a place is named. Pilbara / Port Hedland remains the proposed industrial coast because those plants and that port exist. It is not selected. Arnhem / Gove is not vacant for us. Woomera is not a Starship berth on this page.

Sonic boom, debris, downrange safety, and environmental law are the body of catch, not a waiver copied from another country. No is a complete answer.

Relation to orbit

If orbital compute flies, someone still brings hardware home and sends it again. Radiators and GPUs do not refurbish themselves. Catch is the ground loop for whatever leaves. CrystalCore remains the local brain for delay. It is not a crane.
