# Technical Brief — CrystalCore as Multiplanetary Intelligence Layer

**TerAustralis Incognita**

---

## Design Premise

Multiplanetary operations impose constraints that current Earth-centric intelligence architectures handle poorly:

- High and variable latency
- Intermittent or contested connectivity
- Long periods of isolation
- The need for local decision-making that remains coherent with human intent and continuity

CrystalCore is designed from first principles for this regime.

---

## Core Properties

**Local-first**  
Primary intelligence and memory reside on the local substrate. Continuous external dependency is not required for core function.

**Fail-safe isolation**  
When external links degrade or are compromised, the system defaults to local isolation rather than fail-open behaviour.

**Consent-native**  
Significant actions and modifications to continuity-critical memory require valid, current consent tokens. Consent is architectural, not advisory.

**Continuity-preserving**  
Continuity of decision-making, memory, and agency is treated as a hard constraint. The system is not free to optimise it away.

**Auditable**  
Decision paths and memory operations are designed to remain inspectable by the authorised human operator.

**Distance-as-normal**  
High latency is assumed as the operating condition, not treated as an error state. This matches both deep-outback and interplanetary realities.

---

## Role in the Larger Architecture

CrystalCore is the intelligence counterpart to geographic and industrial redundancy.  
Where Pilbara / Port Hedland supply physical recovery, feedstock, and energy capacity, CrystalCore supplies the decision and continuity layer that can operate when the link back to denser networks is thin or broken.

It is not intended to replace large centralised models for every task. It is intended to provide a sovereign, local, high-integrity core that remains functional under the conditions multiplanetary operations will actually impose.
