## 3. Sydney Basin engravings (public facts)

The Basin holds one of the densest concentrations of Aboriginal petroglyphs in Australia — on the order of two thousand recorded open engraving sites plus shelter pigment sites. Most locations remain unpublished to protect them and because they are sacred. This sitting will not list them.

**Style (public archaeology):** Simple Figurative — outline figures, pecked then abraded. Motifs on the public record include marine animals (whales, sharks, fish, stingrays, turtles), land animals, human figures and footprints (mundoes), objects, and larger ancestral beings. Platforms are typically elevated sandstone with views; fresh water is usually nearby.

**Date:** Grooves are not directly radiocarbon-dated. The style is generally associated with the last several thousand years. Occupation of the region is much older. Treat dates as provisional.

**Living status:** Not prehistoric relics. They remain cultural landscape for Dharawal, Gai-mariagal, Dharug and neighbouring peoples. Early colonial disruption left ethnographic explanation incomplete. Knowledge holders are the authority where they choose to speak.

**Culture-hero types (research labels, not identifications on this page):** Baiame-type (frontal, often outstretched arms); Daramulan-type (often profile, sometimes emu-associated). Densities vary across the Basin. We do not pin a name to an unpublished platform.

## 4. One public site: Jibbon / Djeeban

Jibbon Head, Royal National Park, near Bundeena. Dharawal name recorded in public material as Djeeban / Djeebahn (“sand bars at low tide”). The most extensive engraving complex in the park that is **signposted** for visitors. Motifs on the public interpretive record include whales (including overlapping orcas), stingray, turtle, wallaby/kangaroo, and a human/ancestral figure.

A viewing platform and boardwalk were installed so feet stay off the rock. NSW National Parks and Wildlife Service records conservation work with Local Aboriginal Land Council and community. That is care, not a tour product of this sitting.

We do not publish a site map. We do not use Jibbon as a Catch or Starline plate.
